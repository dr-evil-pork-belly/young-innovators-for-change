Teacher Guide

The Market Year · Grade 6 · 36 weeks

Answers, the point of each week, and the specific wrong turn each one produces. Written for a teacher who has never run an enterprise program and does not intend to become an expert in one.

Where a question is a judgment call this guide says so rather than inventing a correct answer to mark against, and this year has more of those than either of the two before it: a pricing decision has no right answer until you know the team's own cost.

All 163 numeric answers below are recomputed from the problem as stated in the workbook by verify.py, which ships beside this file and runs on every build.

Trimester 1 · Weeks 1 to 12

Read the market

The comparison tools, worked on other people's markets: ratio, unit rate, percent, and the statistics that say whether a market is steady or wild. Nothing of the students' own is designed until week 13. 6.RP does most of the work, and week 5 brings the first negative number this line has ever printed.

Week 1Two Numbers, One Ratio

Ratio & Rate · sits beside: Ratios and ratio language (6.RP.1)
The point

A total says how you did. A ratio says how you did against somebody, and it survives a quiet day when a total does not.

Answers and marking
Warm-up3 to 2
Ratio a3 to 2
Ratio b3 to 5
Ratio c1 to 1
Ratio d5 to 2
Ratio e1 to 3
Watch for

Students write 12:8 and stop. Simplifying is not decoration here: 3 to 2 is the form that stays the same when the market gets busier, which is the whole reason to use a ratio.

Challenge Zone

Nothing changed about the ratio and everything changed about the totals. A student who says the ratio measures them against the other stall while the total measures the day has it.

The point

Watch for

Dividing by the cost instead of the price. That is markup, and it is next week. Here the denominator is always the price.

Challenge Zone

Not $4.20. A 40 percent margin means cost is 60 percent of the price, so price is $3.00 divided by 0.6, which is $5.00. Students who add 40 percent to the cost have computed a markup.

Week 4Markup Is Not Margin

Money · sits beside: Percent of a quantity (6.RP.3c)
The point

Watch for

Challenge Zone

Week 10Revenue Is a Formula

Rules · sits beside: Writing and evaluating expressions (6.EE.9)
The point

Revenue becomes a rule you can put numbers into, which is what makes a what-if question possible at all.

Answers and marking
Revenue a$42.00
Revenue b$50.00
Revenue c$36.00
Revenue d$70.00
Revenue e$0.00
Row e profit−$12.00
Challenge, profit on row a$13.20
Watch for

Students treat revenue as profit. It is the money in the box before anything has come out, and row e exists to make that unmissable.

Challenge Zone

Profit = p x q - c x q - f. On row a that is 42.00 - 16.80 - 12.00 = $13.20. Students who write p x q - c - f have taken the cost off once instead of once per item.

Week 11Change the Price, Change the Line

Rules · sits beside: Dependent and independent variables (6.EE.9)
The point

Watch for

Challenge Zone

Trimester 2 · Weeks 13 to 24

Take a position

The category is assigned in week 13 and at least one other team has it. Everything from there is decided against somebody else: the corner they choose, the price they set, the discount they consider, and the share they aim at.

The two rounds, and the week between them

Weeks 29 and 34 are real selling. In week 33 every team sees every other team's round one prices, sales and share, and decides what to do: match, undercut, hold, or change what they sell. Everybody decides at once.

This needs at least four teams. With two, the response round is a staring contest. Nobody has run this yet, so treat that as a judgment rather than a tested minimum.

The rounds do not need two separate days. Two halves of one market with a break between works, and so do two lunch periods.

The money is play money, in a closed classroom economy, exactly as in Grades 4 and 5. The class bank lends every team the same startup fund, they buy materials from a class supply table, sell and make their own change, then repay the fund. What is left is profit, and this year it can be negative.
